Privacy Policy

yt-autoclip · Last updated November 7, 2026

yt-autoclip is a personal tool run by the owner of the Plot Twist Daily YouTube channel. It is used only with the owner's own Google account.

What it accesses

  • YouTube upload access (youtube.upload): to upload and schedule videos to the owner's channel.
  • YouTube read access (youtube.readonly): to list the channel's own videos and read their public statistics (views, likes, comment counts).

How data is used and stored

The sign-in token and video statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer, to schedule uploads and decide which future videos to make. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data about viewers or other YouTube users is collected beyond public counts on the owner's own videos.

yt-autoclip's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections. Deleting the local files removes all stored data.
